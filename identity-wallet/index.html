Why Choose DigiID?

Next-generation infrastructure for the self-sovereign web. We give power back to the user.

Decentralized Storage

Your data isn't sitting in a vulnerable central server. Credentials are encrypted and pinned to IPFS, secured by the Polygon blockchain.

Zero Knowledge Proofs

Prove you are over 18 without revealing your date of birth. ZK-SNARKs enable ultimate privacy in credential sharing.

1-Second Verification
